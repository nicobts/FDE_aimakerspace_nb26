- Endpoint: top 5 chunks, one answer call, decline gate at dense cosine < 0.45

- Embeddings: the index was built with `qwen/qwen3-embedding-8b` through OpenRouter; queries in this run were embedded locally by the same model quantised in Ollama (CPU copy, `num_gpu 0`). ## What the numbers say

Points 1-6 were written on the 13 drafted questions (run 2026-11-03_1111): hybrid agent 11/13,
hybrid endpoint 11/13 (all gold 10/12), BM25 agent 10/13, dense agent 8/13, wiki agent 1/13,
BM25 endpoint 8/13. Point 7 adds the five assumed questions; the tables above cover all 18.

Read with the traces in `use_case/data/runs/tc3_compare_2026-11-03_1111.json`.

1. **Hybrid wins at both layers.** Ranking only: every gold paper in the top 5 for 11 of 12 answerable
   questions, against 10 for dense and 9 for BM25. With the model: 11 of 13 for the hybrid agent and
   for the hybrid endpoint, against 10 (BM25 agent) and 8 (dense agent). RRF did not fall below its
   better parent on these labels.
2. **The endpoint matches the best agent with one call.** One retrieval and one model call per question
   gives the same 11 of 13 as the agentic loop, and cites every gold paper more often (10 of 12 against
   9 of 12). It also answers the Italian question q03, which every agent answered without searching:
   the endpoint always retrieves, an agent may decide not to.
3. **The wiki agent fails with a small model.** 1 of 13 (the decline). It sends whole sentences to
   `grep_wiki` as a regular expression, gets "(no matches)", and stops after one or two calls; it never
   uses `list_pages` or `read_page`. The same tools passed 9 of 13 with Claude Sonnet in the partial run
   of 2026-11-02 (stopped by credit, not comparable row by row). Direct corpus interaction needs a model
   that plans several steps.
4. **Failures after retrieval are the model's.** q06 (both gold papers reached the model, only the
   Uganda study was used, Rwanda dropped) is multi-hop synthesis. q11 is correct in substance but never
   says "americano"; the label was not changed after seeing the answer.
5. **Finding during the run: the passage cut.** The first six BM25 answers cited the right paper and said
   the results were "cut off". 2,179 of 2,398 chunks are longer than the 1,000 characters Session 5 showed
   per hit (median 1,582, 90th percentile 2,146), and abstracts put results last. Raising the cut to
   2,500 took the BM25 agent from 1 to 3 passes on the same six questions (q01 and q05 flipped), and to
   10 of 13 overall. The rows before the fix are in
   `use_case/data/runs/tc3_checkpoint_before_passage_fix.jsonl`.
6. **The decline works in two layers.** q10 (the company's own lab data) scores cosine 0.70, above the
   0.45 gate, because acrylamide is everywhere in the corpus: the prompt declines it, on both endpoint
   paths, with no figures and no sources.
7. **The five assumed questions (u01-u05) confirm the endpoint and expose false declines.** The hybrid
   endpoint passed 4 of 5 and cited every gold paper; every agent answered u01 (Italian) and u04 (NIR)
   **without searching**, and on u04 the hybrid agent claimed "no evidence in the corpus" when the
   gold paper is there. An agent that skips retrieval turns into a false decline, which a researcher
   would believe. The endpoint's u01 answer is right in substance but never says "300"; the label
   was not changed after seeing it.
